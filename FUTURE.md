# GarageLedger — Future Vision (Cicibyte Corp)

This document outlines Cicibyte Corp’s forward-looking roadmap for GarageLedger. These items are intentionally not part of the current offline-first v1 delivery, but they define the product direction.

## Membership & Licensing

- Online membership system (account-based access)
- Central license validation server (seat-based licensing)
- Offline grace period with periodic re-validation
- License tiers and renewal workflows

## SaaS Model

GarageLedger can evolve into a hybrid model:

- Basic: single device, offline-first, core inventory + analytics
- Pro: multi-device sync, team workflows, advanced reports
- Enterprise: dedicated support, custom integrations, on-prem licensing options

## Mobile Apps & Cloud Sync

- iOS (.ipa) and Android (.apk) companion apps
- Cloud synchronization of inventory and analytics
- Role-based access (owner / manager / staff)
- Optional “offline capture” on mobile with conflict-safe sync

## AI Insights

- AI-driven pricing and investment suggestions based on historical sales
- Seasonality detection and anomaly alerts (e.g., margin drops)
- Smart recommendations: best categories, fastest-selling models, optimal buy ranges
- Natural-language summaries for monthly and quarterly performance

## Integrations (Future)

- Accounting exports and integrations (CSV/Excel + API-based connectors)
- Dealer/marketplace listing integrations (publish inventory to platforms)
- Notification channels (email/WhatsApp/Slack) for key events and alerts
